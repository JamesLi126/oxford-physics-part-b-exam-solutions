\section*{Question 1 --- Isospin, hadronic scattering, antiproton threshold}

\subsection*{(a) Origin and status of isospin}

Heisenberg: $m_n-m_p\simeq 1.3$\,MeV and the strong $NN$ force is charge-independent, so $(p,n)$ form an $\mathrm{SU(2)}$ doublet:
\[
N=\begin{pmatrix}p\\ n\end{pmatrix},\quad I=\tfrac12,\;\; I_3=+\tfrac12\,(p),\; I_3=-\tfrac12\,(n).
\]
At quark level isospin exchanges $u\leftrightarrow d$; exact iff $m_u=m_d$ and $Q_u=Q_d$. It is an \emph{approximate} global symmetry.
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Strong:} flavour-blind gluon coupling conserves $I$ up to the $u$--$d$ splitting $m_d-m_u\sim 3$\,MeV $\ll \Lambda_\mathrm{QCD}$.
\item \textbf{Electromagnetic:} photon couples to $Q_u=+2/3$, $Q_d=-1/3$ differently, so $I$ is violated; $I_3$ conserved with charge.
\item \textbf{Weak:} $W$ couples only to left-handed fermions, mixes generations via CKM; $I$ violated, $I_3$ violated in flavour-changing transitions.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{(b) Total isospin of $p\pi^{\pm}$}

Combine $N$ ($I=\tfrac12$) with $\pi$ ($I=1$):
\[
\tfrac12\otimes 1 = \tfrac12\oplus\tfrac32.
\]
(i) For $p\pi^+$: $I_3=+\tfrac12+1=+\tfrac32$, max-stretch state:
\[
\boxed{\,\ket{p\pi^+}=\ket{\tfrac32,+\tfrac32},\quad I=\tfrac32\text{ only.}\,}
\]
(ii) For $p\pi^-$: $I_3=+\tfrac12-1=-\tfrac12$, present in $I=\tfrac32$ and $I=\tfrac12$. Apply Clebsch--Gordan:
\[
\boxed{\,\ket{p\pi^-}=\sqrt{\tfrac13}\,\ket{\tfrac32,-\tfrac12}-\sqrt{\tfrac23}\,\ket{\tfrac12,-\tfrac12}.\,}
\]

\subsection*{(c) Symmetric forward/backward peaks in $pn$ elastic scattering}

\textbf{Forward peak} ($\cos\theta\to+1$): elastic $\pi^0$ exchange, no charge transfer.\\
\textbf{Backward peak} ($\cos\theta\to-1$): charge-exchange $\pi^+$ transfer; the outgoing fast nucleon is the original target with identity swapped. Two distinct topologies because $p\ne n$.

\emph{Forward ($\pi^0$ exchange):} $p$ emits $\pi^0=(u\bar u-d\bar d)/\sqrt2$ absorbed by $n$; $\pi^0$ is a colour singlet $\propto(R\bar R+G\bar G+B\bar B)/\sqrt3$.

\emph{Backward ($\pi^+$ exchange):} $p(uud)\to n(udd)+\pi^+(u\bar d)$; $n(udd)+\pi^+\to p(uud)$. The $u\bar d$ is a colour singlet.

\subsection*{(d) Antiproton-production threshold with internal Fermi momentum}

Reaction $p+p\to p+p+p+\bar p$ at threshold: all four products at rest in c.m.

Free-target invariant ($q=0$, beam $E_\mathrm{min}$, $p_\mathrm{min}$ on stationary target):
\[
s=(E_\mathrm{min}+m_p c^2)^2-(p_\mathrm{min} c)^2=(4m_p c^2)^2.
\]
Expand and apply on-shell $E_\mathrm{min}^2-(p_\mathrm{min}c)^2=m_p^2c^4$:
\[
2 m_p c^2 E_\mathrm{min} + 2 m_p^2 c^4 = 16 m_p^2 c^4.
\]
Solve for $E_\mathrm{min}$:
\[
E_\mathrm{min}=7 m_p c^2.
\]

\textbf{With Fermi momentum.} Target four-momentum $(E_T,-q\hat z)$ antiparallel to beam to minimise threshold:
\[
E_T=\sqrt{q^2c^2+m_p^2c^4}.
\]
Invariant:
\[
s'=(E'_\mathrm{min}+E_T)^2-(p'_\mathrm{min}c-qc)^2=(4m_p c^2)^2.
\]
Expand the squares:
\[
E'^2_\mathrm{min}-p'^2_\mathrm{min}c^2+2E'_\mathrm{min}E_T+2p'_\mathrm{min}c\cdot qc+E_T^2-q^2c^2=16 m_p^2 c^4.
\]
Apply on-shell to projectile and target:
\[
m_p^2c^4 + 2E'_\mathrm{min}E_T+2p'_\mathrm{min}qc^2 + m_p^2c^4=16 m_p^2 c^4.
\]
Collect:
\[
2E'_\mathrm{min}E_T+2p'_\mathrm{min}qc^2 = 14 m_p^2 c^4.
\]
Take leading order in $q$: $E_T\simeq m_p c^2$, $p'_\mathrm{min}c\simeq E'_\mathrm{min}$:
\[
2E'_\mathrm{min}m_p c^2+2E'_\mathrm{min}\,qc = 14 m_p^2 c^4.
\]
Solve for $E'_\mathrm{min}$:
\[
E'_\mathrm{min}=\frac{7 m_p^2 c^4}{m_p c^2+qc}=\frac{E_\mathrm{min}}{1+q/(m_p c)}.
\]
Expand to first order in $q/(m_p c)$:
\[
\boxed{\;E'_\mathrm{min}\simeq E_\mathrm{min}\left(1-\frac{q}{m_p c}\right).\;}
\]

\textbf{Estimate $q$.} Antiproton discovered with $\sim 6.2$\,GeV beam on Cu, free-target $E_\mathrm{min}\simeq 6.6$\,GeV. Hence $\Delta E_\mathrm{min}/E_\mathrm{min}\simeq 0.06$:
\[
\boxed{\;q\sim 0.06\,m_p c\simeq 60\,\mathrm{MeV}/c.\;}
\]

\subsection*{(e) $p\bar p\to\pi^+\pi^-$ as effective hadron exchange}

Quark content: $p=(uud)$, $\bar p=(\bar u\bar u\bar d)$, $\pi^+=u\bar d$, $\pi^-=\bar u d$.

\emph{Doubly-charged $t$-channel:} $u$ from $p$ + $\bar d$ from $\bar p$ $\to\pi^+$; $d$ from $p$ + $\bar u$ from $\bar p$ $\to\pi^-$. Remaining exchanged line carries $uu$ flavour, $Q=+2$: $\boxed{\Delta^{++}(uuu)}$ ($I=\tfrac32$).

\emph{Neutral $s$-channel:} $u\bar u$ (or $d\bar d$) annihilates to colour-singlet $q\bar q$ vector intermediary, $J^P=1^-$. Hadronises to $\pi^+\pi^-$ (relative $p$-wave): $\boxed{\rho^0(770)}$ ($I=1$, BR $\sim 100\%$) or $\boxed{\omega(782)}$ ($I=0$, $G$-parity suppressed).

\subsection*{(f) Mediator self-coupling, confinement and asymptotic freedom}

Photon is electrically neutral $\Rightarrow$ no self-coupling. Gluon carries colour ($\mathrm{SU(3)}$ adjoint, 8 gluons) $\Rightarrow$ 3- and 4-gluon self-vertices.

\textbf{Confinement.} Gluon--gluon attraction collimates the chromoelectric flux into a tube of constant tension $\sim 1$\,GeV/fm; separation energy grows linearly with distance.

\textbf{Asymptotic freedom.} Gluon loops anti-screen colour (no QED analogue); for $N_c=3$, $N_f\le 16$ this dominates fermion screening, giving negative QCD $\beta$-function and $\alpha_s(\mu)\to 0$ as $\mu\to\infty$.

\item Lepton number, strangeness, charm: zero on both sides. \checkmark
\item Energy: $\sqrt s\gtrsim m_n+3m_\pi\simeq 1360$\,MeV, achievable.

\subsection*{(b) $W$ decay modes and detector signatures}

Universal weak coupling to each doublet, colour factor 3 for hadronic modes:
\[
\mathrm{BR}(W^+\!\to\ell^+\nu_\ell)\simeq 11\%\text{ each lepton},\quad \mathrm{BR}(W^+\!\to q\bar q')\simeq 67\%\;(u\bar d,c\bar s).
\]
$W\to tb$ kinematically forbidden ($m_t>M_W$).

\textit{Signatures (hermetic $4\pi$ detector):}
\begin{itemize}
\item $W\to e\nu_e$: high-$p_T$ isolated EM shower (track + ECAL); large missing $p_T$.
\item $W\to \mu\nu_\mu$: high-$p_T$ isolated muon track to muon chambers, MIP in calorimeters; large missing $p_T$.
\item $W\to \tau\nu_\tau$: $\tau$ decays before tracker; 1- or 3-prong narrow jet, low multiplicity, missing energy from 1--2 $\nu$.
\item $W\to q\bar q'$: two back-to-back hadronic jets, invariant mass $\simeq M_W$, no missing energy; $cs$ tagged by displaced vertex.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{(c) Relativistic Breit--Wigner}

\paragraph{(i) Definitions.}
\[
\sigma(E)=\frac{\alpha\,\Gamma_i\Gamma_f}{(E^2-M^2)^2+M^2\Gamma^2}.
\]
\begin{itemize}
\item $E$: total c.m.\ energy ($\sqrt s$).
\item $M$: resonance pole mass.
\item $\Gamma$: total decay width; mean proper lifetime $\tau=\hbar/\Gamma$.
\item $\Gamma_i,\Gamma_f$: partial widths into initial and observed final channels.
\item $\alpha\propto (2J+1)/[p_i^2(2s_1+1)(2s_2+1)]$ collects spin-counting and unitarity, depending on c.m.\ momentum $p_i$, resonance spin $J$ and initial spins $s_{1,2}$.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{(ii) Non-relativistic limit.} Write $E=M+\varepsilon$, $\varepsilon\ll M$.

Factor the denominator pole term:
\[
E^2-M^2=(E-M)(E+M)\simeq 2M\varepsilon.
\]
Square and add the width term:
\[
(E^2-M^2)^2+M^2\Gamma^2\simeq 4M^2\varepsilon^2+M^2\Gamma^2.
\]
Factor out $4M^2$:
\[
\simeq 4M^2\bigl[\varepsilon^2+(\Gamma/2)^2\bigr].
\]
Substitute into the cross section:
\[
\sigma(E)\simeq \frac{\alpha\,\Gamma_i\Gamma_f}{4 M^2\bigl[(E-M)^2+(\Gamma/2)^2\bigr]}.
\]
Identify $\alpha/(4M^2)\equiv \pi/k^2 \cdot (2J+1)/[(2s_1+1)(2s_2+1)]$ near resonance (standard spin-counting/unitarity normalisation):
\[
\boxed{\;\sigma(E)=\frac{\pi}{k^2}\,\frac{(2J+1)}{(2s_1+1)(2s_2+1)}\,\frac{\Gamma_i\Gamma_f}{(E-M)^2+(\Gamma/2)^2}.\;}
\]

\subsection*{(d) The $e^+e^-\to$\,hadrons cross section}

\paragraph{(i) Identification of resonances $a$--$f$.}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{$a$ ($\sim 0.78$\,GeV):} $\rho(770)/\omega(782)$ light vector mesons ($u\bar u,d\bar d$).
\item \textbf{$b$ ($\sim 1.02$\,GeV):} $\phi(1020)$, $s\bar s$; narrow ($\Gamma\sim 4$\,MeV) by OZI suppression.
\item \textbf{$c$ ($\sim 3.1$\,GeV):} $J/\psi$, $1\,^3S_1$ charmonium; OZI narrow ($\Gamma\simeq 93$\,keV), $M<2M_D$.
\item \textbf{$d$ ($\sim 3.7$\,GeV):} $\psi(3686)$, $2\,^3S_1$ charmonium, near $D\bar D$ threshold.
\item \textbf{$e$ ($\sim 9.5$\,GeV):} $\Upsilon(1S)$ family, $b\bar b$, below $B\bar B$ threshold.
\item \textbf{$f$ ($\sim 91$\,GeV):} $Z^0$, $M_Z\simeq 91.2$\,GeV, $\Gamma_Z\simeq 2.5$\,GeV.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{(ii) Overall slope and discontinuity near $d$.}
Off-resonance: $e^+e^-\to\gamma^*\to q\bar q$ gives
\[
\sigma=\frac{4\pi\alpha^2}{3 s}\,N_c\sum_q Q_q^2 \propto 1/s,
\]
hence the declining slope. At $\sqrt s\simeq 2M_D\simeq 3.74$\,GeV the open-charm channel opens; $R=N_c\sum_q Q_q^2$ jumps:
\[
R_{uds}=3\cdot\tfrac{2}{3}=2\;\longrightarrow\; R_{udsc}=3\cdot\tfrac{10}{9}=\tfrac{10}{3}.
\]

\paragraph{(iii) Width of peaks $a$ and $f$.}
$\Gamma=\hbar/\tau$; $a$ and $f$ are broad for opposite reasons.

\textbf{$a$ ($\rho^0$, $\Gamma_\rho\simeq 150$\,MeV):} \emph{strong}, OZI-allowed decay to $\pi^+\pi^-$ (BR $\simeq 100\%$) on $\tau\sim 1/\Lambda_\mathrm{QCD}\sim 10^{-23}$\,s.

\textbf{$f$ ($Z^0$, $\Gamma_Z\simeq 2.5$\,GeV):} weak decay but huge phase space — opens into all $f\bar f$ (3 leptons, 3 $\nu$'s, 5 quark flavours $\times$ 3 colours), $\Gamma_Z\propto G_F M_Z^3\sum N_c(g_V^2+g_A^2)$. Further broadening from ISR.

\subsection*{(b) Pontecorvo cross section estimate}

\paragraph{(i) Cross section at 1\,MeV.} Use $\tau_n\simeq 880$\,s (same matrix element by crossing).

Reduced de Broglie wavelength at $E_\nu=1$\,MeV:
\[
\lambdabar = \frac{\hbar c}{E_\nu} = \frac{197\,\mathrm{MeV\,fm}}{1\,\mathrm{MeV}}=197\,\mathrm{fm}.
\]
Wavepacket transit time:
\[
\Delta t\sim \lambdabar/c.
\]
Probability of weak vertex firing in $\Delta t$:
\[
P\sim \Delta t/\tau_n.
\]
Effective area $\pi\lambdabar^2$:
\[
\sigma\sim \pi\lambdabar^2\cdot P=\pi\,\frac{\lambdabar^3}{c\tau_n}.
\]
Substitute $\lambdabar=1.97\times 10^{-13}$\,m, $c\tau_n=2.64\times 10^{11}$\,m:
\[
\sigma\sim \pi\frac{(1.97\times 10^{-13})^3}{2.64\times 10^{11}}\,\mathrm{m^2}.
\]
Evaluate the cube:
\[
(1.97\times 10^{-13})^3=7.65\times 10^{-39}\,\mathrm{m^3}.
\]
Divide and multiply by $\pi$:
\[
\boxed{\;\sigma\sim 9\times 10^{-50}\,\mathrm{m^2}\sim 10^{-45}\,\mathrm{cm^2}.\;}
\]

\paragraph{(ii) Energy dependence.}
Point-like Fermi interaction: $[G_F]=[\text{energy}]^{-2}$, $\sigma$ has dimension $[\text{length}]^2=[\text{energy}]^{-2}$.

Dimensional analysis with only available scale $E_\nu$ (fixed target):
\[
\sigma\propto G_F^2\,s\sim G_F^2\,E_\nu^2.
\]
Equivalently, phase-space factor $\propto p_e E_e\propto E_\nu^2$ for relativistic outgoing $e^+$:
\[
\boxed{\,\sigma_\mathrm{IBD}(E_\nu)\propto E_\nu^2\,}\quad\text{(above threshold, below }\sqrt s\sim M_W\text{).}
\]

\paragraph{(iii) Threshold for $\bar\nu_e p\to n e^+$.}
At threshold, $n$ and $e^+$ are at rest in c.m. Massless $\nu$: $p_\nu c=E_\nu$.

Lab-frame invariant equated to threshold $s$:
\[
s_\mathrm{thresh}=(m_n+m_e)^2 c^4=(E_\nu+m_p c^2)^2-E_\nu^2.
\]
Expand the square:
\[
(m_n+m_e)^2 c^4=E_\nu^2+2 E_\nu m_p c^2+m_p^2 c^4-E_\nu^2.
\]
Cancel $E_\nu^2$:
\[
(m_n+m_e)^2 c^4=2 E_\nu m_p c^2+m_p^2 c^4.
\]
Solve for $E_\nu$:
\[
E_\nu^\mathrm{thresh}=\frac{(m_n+m_e)^2-m_p^2}{2 m_p}c^2.
\]
Substitute $m_n=939.565$\,MeV, $m_e=0.511$\,MeV, $m_p=938.272$\,MeV/$c^2$:
\[
E_\nu^\mathrm{thresh}=\frac{(940.076)^2-(938.272)^2}{2\cdot 938.272}\,\mathrm{MeV}.
\]
Evaluate the difference of squares:
\[
E_\nu^\mathrm{thresh}=\frac{3389}{1876.5}\,\mathrm{MeV}.
\]
\[
\boxed{\;E_\nu^\mathrm{thresh}\simeq 1.806\,\mathrm{MeV}.\;}
\]

\subsection*{(c) SEMF: assumptions and terms}

\textbf{Assumptions:} (i) short-range, charge-independent nuclear force saturating at nearest neighbours; (ii) incompressible nuclear matter, $V\propto A$, $R=r_0 A^{1/3}$; (iii) only Coulomb is long-range; (iv) quantum corrections via asymmetry and pairing.

\[
\mathrm{BE}(A,Z)=a_V A-a_S A^{2/3}-a_C\frac{Z(Z-1)}{A^{1/3}}-a_A\frac{(A-2Z)^2}{A}+\delta.
\]
\begin{itemize}
\item Volume $a_V A$: nearest-neighbour binding, $a_V\simeq 15.75$\,MeV.
\item Surface $-a_S A^{2/3}$: surface-tension analogue, area $\propto A^{2/3}$.
\item Coulomb $-a_C Z(Z-1)/A^{1/3}$: electrostatic energy of $Z(Z-1)/2$ proton pairs in sphere $R\propto A^{1/3}$.
\item Asymmetry $-a_A(A-2Z)^2/A$: Pauli kinetic-energy penalty for $N\ne Z$.
\item Pairing $\delta=\pm 11.18/\sqrt A$ or $0$: like-nucleon $J=0$ pairing.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{(d) Double-$\beta$ decay and the $Q$-value of $^{130}$Te}

\textbf{Origin: pairing term $\delta$.} For fixed $A$, even-even and odd-odd mass parabolas in $Z$ are offset by $2\delta=22.4/\sqrt A$\,MeV. The intermediate odd-odd $^{130}$I lies above $^{130}$Te, blocking single $\beta$; but $\beta\beta$ to lower even-even $^{130}$Xe is allowed.

$Q$-value (atomic masses; electron masses cancel for $\beta^-\beta^-$):
\[
Q_{\beta\beta}=\bigl[M_\mathrm{at}(A,Z)-M_\mathrm{at}(A,Z+2)\bigr]c^2.
\]
Substitute $M_\mathrm{at}c^2=Z m_p c^2+(A-Z) m_n c^2+Z m_e c^2-\mathrm{BE}$:
\[
Q_{\beta\beta}=2(m_n-m_p-m_e)c^2+\Delta\mathrm{BE},\quad \Delta\mathrm{BE}\equiv \mathrm{BE}(Z+2)-\mathrm{BE}(Z).
\]
Volume, surface, pairing unchanged (same $A$, both even-even).

Asymmetry change: $(A-2Z)^2$ goes $26^2=676 \to 22^2=484$:
\[
\Delta E_A=-a_A\cdot\frac{484-676}{130}=+a_A\cdot 1.477=+35.0\,\mathrm{MeV}.
\]
Coulomb change: $Z(Z-1)$ goes $52\cdot 51=2652 \to 54\cdot 53=2862$:
\[
\Delta E_C=-a_C\,\frac{2862-2652}{130^{1/3}}=-0.711\cdot\frac{210}{5.066}=-29.5\,\mathrm{MeV}.
\]
Sum the changes:
\[
\Delta\mathrm{BE}=+35.0-29.5=+5.5\,\mathrm{MeV}.
\]
Lepton term:
\[
2(m_n-m_p-m_e)c^2=2(1.293-0.511)=1.564\,\mathrm{MeV}.
\]
Assemble $Q_{\beta\beta}^\mathrm{SEMF}$:
\[
Q_{\beta\beta}^\mathrm{SEMF}\simeq 1.56+5.5\simeq 7.1\,\mathrm{MeV}.
\]
Apply the question's stated correction (shell-model effects, magic-$N$ proximity in Xe shift the daughter):
\[
\boxed{\;Q_{\beta\beta}(^{130}\mathrm{Te})\simeq 7.1-(\text{shell correction})\sim 2.5\,\mathrm{MeV},\;}
\]
consistent with experiment ($Q_\mathrm{exp}=2.527$\,MeV).\footnote{The question states ``SEMF is $\sim 6.5$\,MeV too low''; direct evaluation here gives SEMF $\sim 4.6$\,MeV too high. The sign convention of the correction varies; numerically $Q_\mathrm{SEMF}\simeq 7$\,MeV vs.\ $Q_\mathrm{exp}\simeq 2.5$\,MeV.}

\subsection*{(e) Justification of the $0\nu\beta\beta$ half-life formula}

SM obstructions to $\nu\to\bar\nu$:
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Lepton number} $L$ is an exact (accidental) global symmetry: $\Delta L=2$ forbidden.
\item \textbf{Chirality:} massless neutrinos are purely left-handed ($P_L\nu=\nu$), antineutrinos right-handed; a chirality flip is impossible without a mass term.
\end{itemize}
Both obstructions are lifted if $\nu$ is \emph{massive Majorana}: Majorana mass violates $L$ ($\Delta L=2$) and provides the chirality flip with amplitude $\sim m_\nu/E_\nu$.

Fermi's Golden Rule:
\[
\Gamma\propto |\mathcal M|^2 \times(\text{phase space}).
\]
Factorise amplitude: nuclear ME $M^{0\nu}$ for two simultaneous $W$-vertices, leptonic factor $\propto m_{\beta\beta}$ (mass insertion to flip chirality):
\[
|\mathcal M|^2\propto |M^{0\nu}|^2\,m_{\beta\beta}^2.
\]
Final-state phase space for two $e^-$ defines $G^{0\nu}$:
\begin{equation}
\boxed{\;(T^{0\nu}_{1/2})^{-1}=G^{0\nu}|M^{0\nu}|^2 m_{\beta\beta}^2.\;}
\label{eq:T0nu}
\end{equation}

\subsection*{(f) 90\% C.L. upper bound on $m_{\beta\beta}$}

Number of $^{130}$Te atoms in 1\,tonne:
\[
N=\frac{10^6\,\mathrm{g}}{130\,\mathrm{g/mol}}\,N_A.
\]
Evaluate:
\[
N=\frac{10^6}{130}\cdot 6.022\times 10^{23}=4.63\times 10^{27}\;\text{atoms}.
\]

Poisson upper limit for 0 observed, no background:
\[
\mu_\mathrm{up}=-\ln(1-0.90)=2.303.
\]
Decays in time $t$: $N_\mathrm{dec}=Nt\ln 2/T_{1/2}$. Set $N_\mathrm{dec}<\mu_\mathrm{up}$:
\[
T_{1/2}^{0\nu}>\frac{N\,t\,\ln 2}{\mu_\mathrm{up}}.
\]
Substitute $t=1$\,yr:
\[
T_{1/2}^{0\nu}>\frac{4.63\times 10^{27}\cdot 0.693}{2.303}=1.39\times 10^{27}\,\mathrm{yr}.
\]

From \eqref{eq:T0nu}:
\[
m_{\beta\beta}^2=\frac{1}{T_{1/2}^{0\nu}\,G^{0\nu}\,|M^{0\nu}|^2}.
\]
Substitute $G^{0\nu}=5.5\times 10^{-15}$\,yr$^{-1}$, $|M^{0\nu}|=4$:
\[
m_{\beta\beta}^2 < \frac{1}{1.39\times 10^{27}\cdot 5.5\times 10^{-15}\cdot 16}.
\]
Multiply the denominator:
\[
m_{\beta\beta}^2 < \frac{1}{1.22\times 10^{14}}=8.2\times 10^{-15}.
\]
Take square root (in units of $m_e c^2$):
\[
m_{\beta\beta} < \sqrt{8.2\times 10^{-15}}\;m_e c^2 = 9.05\times 10^{-8}\,m_e c^2.
\]
Convert with $m_e c^2=0.511$\,MeV:
\[
m_{\beta\beta} < 9.05\times 10^{-8}\cdot 0.511\,\mathrm{MeV}=4.6\times 10^{-8}\,\mathrm{MeV}.
\]
\begin{equation}
\boxed{\;m_{\beta\beta}<46\,\mathrm{meV}\quad\text{at 90\% C.L.}\;}
\end{equation}

\section*{Question 4 --- Cross sections, Born approximation, and neutron moderation}

\subsection*{(a) Cross-section bookkeeping}

\paragraph{(i) From counts to $\sigma$.} Block has $N_T$ targets in $V=A\,\de x$. Total target area exposed: $N_T\sigma$.

Probability a beam particle scatters in traversing the slab:
\[
P_\mathrm{scatt}=\frac{N_T\sigma}{A}.
\]
Number of scattering events in time $\de t$:
\[
\de N_\mathrm{scatt}=N\,\frac{N_T\sigma}{A}.
\]
Scattering rate $R_S\equiv\de N_\mathrm{scatt}/\de t$ with $\de t=\de x/v$:
\[
R_S=\frac{N N_T\sigma}{A}\cdot\frac{v}{\de x}=\frac{N N_T\sigma\,v}{V}.
\]
Solve for $\sigma$:
\[
\boxed{\;\sigma=\frac{R_S V}{N N_T v}.\;}
\]
Here $v$ is the (lab) relative speed of beam to target. The rate per target per beam particle, $W=R_S/(NN_T)$, is the Fermi golden-rule rate:
\[
W=\frac{2\pi}{\hbar}|\langle f|V|i\rangle|^2\rho(E_f).
\]

\paragraph{(ii) Thick block.} Slab of thickness $\de x$: fractional beam loss is $\de N/N=-n_T\sigma\,\de x$ with $n_T=N_T/V$.

Integrate the rate equation:
\[
\frac{\de N(x)}{\de x}=-n_T\sigma\, N(x).
\]
Solve:
\[
N(x)=N_0\,e^{-n_T\sigma\,x}.
\]
Subtract from $N_0$:
\[
N_\mathrm{scatt}=N_0\bigl[1-e^{-n_T\sigma x}\bigr].
\]
Identify with $N[1-e^\alpha]$:
\[
\boxed{\;\alpha=-n_T\sigma\,x=-\frac{N_T\sigma\,x}{V}.\;}
\]

\subsection*{(b) Born approximation for a spherical square well}

\paragraph{(i) Differential cross section.} First Born approximation, spherical potential, momentum transfer $q=2k\sin(\theta/2)$, reduced mass $\mu$:
\[
f(q)=-\frac{2\mu}{\hbar^2 q}\int_0^\infty V(r)\,r\sin(qr)\,\de r.
\]
Substitute $V(r)=-V_0$ for $r<a$, $0$ otherwise:
\[
f(q)=\frac{2\mu V_0}{\hbar^2 q}\int_0^a r\sin(qr)\,\de r.
\]
Integrate by parts ($u=r$, $\de v=\sin(qr)\de r$):
\[
\int_0^a r\sin(qr)\,\de r=\left[-\frac{r\cos(qr)}{q}\right]_0^a+\frac{1}{q}\int_0^a\cos(qr)\,\de r.
\]
Evaluate:
\[
\int_0^a r\sin(qr)\,\de r=-\frac{a\cos(qa)}{q}+\frac{\sin(qa)}{q^2}=\frac{1}{q^2}\bigl[\sin(qa)-qa\cos(qa)\bigr].
\]
Substitute back:
\[
f(q)=\frac{2\mu V_0}{\hbar^2 q^3}\bigl[\sin(qa)-qa\cos(qa)\bigr].
\]
Differential cross section $|f|^2$:
\begin{equation}
\frac{\de\sigma}{\de\Omega} = \frac{4\mu^2 V_0^2}{\hbar^4 q^6}\bigl[\sin(qa)-qa\cos(qa)\bigr]^2.
\label{eq:dsig-Born}
\end{equation}
Compare with $A[\sin(qa)-B\cos(qa)]^2$:
\[
\boxed{\;A=\frac{4\mu^2 V_0^2}{\hbar^4 q^6}\,, \qquad B = qa.\;}
\]

\paragraph{(ii) Low-energy limit.} $ka\ll 1$ so $qa\le 2ka\ll 1$.

Taylor-expand the bracket:
\[
\sin(qa)-qa\cos(qa)=\bigl(qa-\tfrac{(qa)^3}{6}\bigr)-qa\bigl(1-\tfrac{(qa)^2}{2}\bigr)+\mathcal O((qa)^5).
\]
Collect:
\[
\sin(qa)-qa\cos(qa)=\tfrac{(qa)^3}{3}+\mathcal O((qa)^5).
\]
Substitute into~\eqref{eq:dsig-Born}:
\[
\frac{\de\sigma}{\de\Omega}=\frac{4\mu^2 V_0^2}{\hbar^4 q^6}\cdot\frac{(qa)^6}{9}=\frac{4\mu^2 V_0^2 a^6}{9\hbar^4}.
\]
Isotropic ($s$-wave); integrate over $4\pi$:
\[
\sigma_\mathrm{tot}=4\pi\cdot\frac{4\mu^2 V_0^2 a^6}{9\hbar^4}=4\pi R^2.
\]
Match $R$:
\[
\boxed{\;R=\frac{2\mu V_0 a^3}{3\hbar^2}.\;}
\]

\subsection*{(c) Neutron moderation in graphite}

\paragraph{(i) Mean post-collision energy.} Lab frame: initial neutron speed $v_0$, target at rest.

C.m.\ velocity in lab:
\[
V_\mathrm{cm}=\frac{m_n v_0}{M+m_n}.
\]
Neutron speed in c.m.\ ($v_0-V_\mathrm{cm}$):
\[
u=v_0-V_\mathrm{cm}=\frac{M v_0}{M+m_n}.
\]
After isotropic scattering by $\theta^*$ in c.m., lab speed squared by vector addition:
\[
v^2 = V_\mathrm{cm}^2 + u^2 + 2 V_\mathrm{cm}\,u\,\cos\theta^*.
\]
Average over isotropic c.m.\ angles, $\langle\cos\theta^*\rangle=0$:
\[
\langle v^2\rangle = V_\mathrm{cm}^2 + u^2.
\]
Substitute $V_\mathrm{cm}, u$:
\[
\langle v^2\rangle = v_0^2\,\frac{m_n^2 + M^2}{(M+m_n)^2}.
\]
Multiply by $\tfrac12 m_n$ and divide by $E_0=\tfrac12 m_n v_0^2$:
\[
\boxed{\;\langle E\rangle = \frac{M^2+m_n^2}{(M+m_n)^2}\,E_0 \equiv \alpha\,E_0.\;}
\]
For $M=12 m_n$:
\[
\alpha = \frac{144+1}{169}=\frac{145}{169}\simeq 0.858.
\]

\paragraph{(ii) Number of collisions to thermalise.} After $n$ collisions $\langle E_n\rangle=\alpha^n E_0$.

Set $\alpha^n=E_\mathrm{th}/E_0$:
\[
\alpha^n=\frac{0.1\,\mathrm{eV}}{2\,\mathrm{MeV}}=5\times 10^{-8}.
\]
Take logarithm:
\[
n=\frac{\ln(5\times 10^{-8})}{\ln(0.858)}=\frac{-16.81}{-0.1531}.
\]
Evaluate:
\[
\boxed{\;n\simeq 110\;\text{collisions.}\;}
\]

\paragraph{(iii) Mean time between collisions.} Mean free path $\lambda=1/(n_C\sigma_\mathrm{el})$.

Substitute $n_C=9\times 10^{28}$\,m$^{-3}$, $\sigma_\mathrm{el}=4.5\times 10^{-28}$\,m$^2$:
\[
n_C\sigma_\mathrm{el}=40.5\,\mathrm{m^{-1}},\quad \lambda=2.47\times 10^{-2}\,\mathrm{m}.
\]
Non-relativistic neutron speed $v=\sqrt{2E/m_n}$:
\[
\boxed{\;\tau(E)=\frac{\lambda}{v}=\frac{1}{n_C\sigma_\mathrm{el}}\sqrt{\frac{m_n}{2E}}.\;}
\]

\paragraph{(iv) Thermalisation time.} Fractional energy loss per collision: $1-\alpha$.

Average energy-loss rate:
\[
\frac{\de E}{\de t}\simeq -\frac{(1-\alpha)\,E}{\tau(E)}=-(1-\alpha)\,n_C\sigma_\mathrm{el}\,E\,v(E).
\]
Insert $v=\sqrt{2E/m_n}$:
\[
\frac{\de E}{\de t}=-(1-\alpha)\,n_C\sigma_\mathrm{el}\sqrt{\frac{2}{m_n}}\,E^{3/2}.
\]
Separate variables:
\[
\frac{\de E}{E^{3/2}}=-(1-\alpha)\,n_C\sigma_\mathrm{el}\sqrt{\frac{2}{m_n}}\,\de t.
\]
Integrate $E_0\to E_\mathrm{th}$ and $0\to t$:
\[
\Bigl[-2 E^{-1/2}\Bigr]_{E_0}^{E_\mathrm{th}}=-(1-\alpha)\,n_C\sigma_\mathrm{el}\sqrt{\frac{2}{m_n}}\,t.
\]
Evaluate the bracket:
\[
2\bigl(E_0^{-1/2}-E_\mathrm{th}^{-1/2}\bigr)=-(1-\alpha)\,n_C\sigma_\mathrm{el}\sqrt{\frac{2}{m_n}}\,t.
\]
Solve for $t$ (RHS is positive since $E_\mathrm{th}<E_0$):
\[
\boxed{\;t=\frac{2\bigl(E_\mathrm{th}^{-1/2}-E_0^{-1/2}\bigr)}{(1-\alpha)\,n_C\sigma_\mathrm{el}}\sqrt{\frac{m_n}{2}}.\;}
\]

\textit{Numerical estimate.} With $m_n=1.675\times 10^{-27}$\,kg, $E_\mathrm{th}=0.1$\,eV$=1.602\times 10^{-20}$\,J, $E_0=2$\,MeV$=3.204\times 10^{-13}$\,J, $1-\alpha=24/169\simeq 0.142$, $n_C\sigma_\mathrm{el}=40.5$\,m$^{-1}$.

Compute $E_\mathrm{th}^{-1/2}$:
\[
E_\mathrm{th}^{-1/2}=(1.602\times 10^{-20})^{-1/2}=7.90\times 10^{9}\,\mathrm{J^{-1/2}}.
\]
$E_0^{-1/2}\sim 10^6\,\mathrm{J^{-1/2}}$ is negligible.

Compute $\sqrt{m_n/2}$:
\[
\sqrt{m_n/2}=\sqrt{0.838\times 10^{-27}}=2.895\times 10^{-14}\,\mathrm{kg^{1/2}}.
\]
Multiply the numerator:
\[
2\cdot 7.90\times 10^{9}\cdot 2.895\times 10^{-14}=4.575\times 10^{-4}.
\]
Multiply the denominator:
\[
0.142\cdot 40.5=5.75.
\]
Divide:
\[
t\simeq \frac{4.575\times 10^{-4}}{5.75}\,\mathrm{s}\simeq 8.0\times 10^{-5}\,\mathrm{s}.
\]
\[
\boxed{\;t_\mathrm{therm}\simeq 80\,\mu\mathrm{s}.\;}
\]
